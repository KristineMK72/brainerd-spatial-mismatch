Product Philosophy

If every tool requires a map, you limit the market. Spatialytics is a small-business operating system first. Spatial intelligence is used only when geography genuinely improves the answer.

“You don’t need 12 enterprise applications. You need one affordable system that understands your business.”

A 10-person Brainerd business cannot stitch Salesforce + ServiceNow + HubSpot + Monday + ArcGIS + Power BI together. Spatialytics is the alternative.

The 20 Modules

Everything a small-business owner needs to run the business every day. GIS appears only where it adds real value.

01

Daily Business Dashboard

TODAY: revenue booked, appointments, jobs, open tasks, new leads, unpaid invoices, employee availability, cancellations, customer issues. Plus Needs Attention: overdue invoices, unassigned jobs, absent staff, uncontacted leads, low inventory.

02

Smart Scheduling

Appointments, employees, shifts, jobs, rooms, equipment, availability, recurring. Auto-detect: double booking, unavailable staff, insufficient gaps, overbooked days, unused capacity.

03

Employee Management

Status, hours, capacity, schedules, availability, PTO, certifications, training, notes, performance, timesheets. Integrate payroll — do not become an HR/payroll company.

04

Time Tracking

Start Shift → Start Job → Break → End Job → End Shift. Owner sees: Scheduled vs Actual vs Overtime vs Billable hours for the day.

05

Job Profitability

Revenue − labor − materials − subcontractors − other = true job profit. Reveal which job types make $600 vs $83.

06

Estimate → Job → Invoice

Full lifecycle: Lead → Estimate → Approved → Scheduled → Job → Completed → Invoice → Paid. One system instead of five spreadsheets.

07

Accounts Receivable Dashboard

Outstanding, due this week, overdue, 90+ days. Morning follow-up list with customer, amount, and days overdue.

08

Sales Pipeline

Lightweight CRM: New Lead → Contacted → Quote Sent → Follow-up → Won/Lost. Pipeline value, conversion rate, average job size, leads this month.

09

“Don’t Forget” Engine

Quote with no response · Customer silent 30 days · Invoice overdue · Contract renewal approaching · Certification expiring · Recurring service missed.

10

Inventory Management

Current · Committed · Available · Reorder point · Supplier · Cost · Selling price. Alert when a product will fall below reorder after tomorrow’s jobs.

11

Equipment Management

Serials, purchase dates, warranties, maintenance, inspections, assigned employee, operating hours. Spatial can show location later.

12

Customer Management

Full profile: contact, services, appointments, purchases, invoices, notes, preferences. Lifetime value, last/next service, risk flags.

13

Customer Retention Engine

Detect at-risk customers: “Normally buys every 30 days — now 58 days.” Generate actionable notifications before churn.

14

Marketing Automation

Welcome · Review request after job · 30-day follow-up · 90-day reactivation · Birthday/anniversary. Business logic, not a Mailchimp clone.

15

Business KPI Dashboard

This month: revenue, expenses, gross profit, new/repeat customers, avg transaction, quotes, conversion, outstanding invoices — with vs-last-month arrows.

16

AI Business Advisor

“Why was revenue down?” → examines data and answers in plain language. “What should I pay attention to today?” → overdue invoices, uncontacted leads, under-utilized staff.

17

SOP / Task Management

Opening & closing checklists, assigned tasks. Capture processes that currently live in someone’s head.

18

Recurring Work Engine

“Jane needs service every 4 weeks” → auto-generate next job, reminder, invoice, follow-up. The business’s memory for service companies.

19

Pricing Intelligence

Enter labor, materials, overhead, desired margin → minimum profitable price + recommended price at target margin.

20

Spatialize Layer

Customers on a map · Routes · Service areas · Competitor density · Market opportunity · Equipment location. Added only after the daily OS is habit.

Build Order (Habit First)

Ship what creates daily dependence first. Spatial comes last.

  1. 1Daily Dashboard + Needs Attention — the morning screen
  2. 2Job Profitability — highest immediate insight per job type
  3. 3Accounts Receivable — cash-flow follow-up every morning
  4. 4Don’t-Forget Engine — the business’s memory
  5. 5Estimate → Job → Invoice — end-to-end lifecycle
  6. 6Smart Scheduling + Time Tracking — capacity and labor truth
  7. 7Customer Retention + Pipeline — growth and churn signals
  8. 8Spatial layer — only after daily habit is established

Spatial Case Study: Brainerd

Why geography still matters — and why it is the differentiator, not the entry ticket.

Crow Wing County has ~532 residents in Computer & Mathematical occupations, yet 74.2% of statewide Comp/Math jobs sit in the Twin Cities metro. Local LQ is low (0.45–0.55 residential; NW MN nonmetro ~0.32). Specialized opportunity is almost unreachable within realistic drive time. This is classic spatial mismatch.
